    How to play

    Each card is a domino with two halves. A half is either your colour, your colour with a gem, or neutral stone.

    1. On your turn, play one of the 3 cards in your hand, then draw a new one. Everyone has a 12-card deck.
    2. The first card must cover the centre square. After that, a card must overlap or touch a card already on the board.
    3. Cards must lie flat: both halves have to land on stacks of the same height. Stacks are at most 3 cards tall.
    4. You can't completely cover a single card. Each new card has to straddle a seam or open ground.

    Only the top of each stack counts. When nobody can play, you score:

    • 1 point per square in your largest connected group (orthogonal)
    • 2 points per gem of yours still visible anywhere

    Ties go to whoever has more visible squares. Use stone to cut rival groups apart.

    Controls

    • Pick a card, hover the board, click to place. R or Shift+scroll rotates. 1–3 select cards.
    • Right-drag orbits the camera, scroll zooms. On touch: tap to preview, then tap Place.
    • Hover a player in the scoreboard to highlight their largest group.
